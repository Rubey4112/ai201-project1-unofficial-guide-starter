**alemonator**: My thoughts from close to far:

1. Honestly I enjoyed a lot of what on campus GMU has to offer. Corner Pocket at the Hub has pool, air hockey, pingpong, arcade/board games, and game console areas you can rent with friends. It's free on Friday nights. If you live on campus, you can also check out board games for free. There's tons of small weekly events like Bob Ross painting and karaoke at the Ridge. There's tons of random people visiting campus all the time: political speakers, cool seminars from Schar basically every day, I believe Tara Strong visited the gaming club a few weeks ago. Plus, every so often GMU will have free food and tshirts at events. Like others said, theres discounts at Eagle Bank (concerts, comedians). Best bet for consistent activities is to join a campus organization.
2. University Mall is a short walk from campus. There's a cat cafe, comic store, and movie theater there. Mason students get a free movie ticket every month!
3. GMU Gunston Go bus takes you to Fair Lakes, Fair Oaks Mall, and Fairfax Corner. There's some cute places in Old Town Fairfax from the CUE bus too.
4. It's also fun to check out the other campuses! Scitech has a cool adventure course and theres tons of stuff in Arlington. (I'm a big fan of the Ballston Quarter which is a metro stop before Arlington if i recall correctly)
5. DC has just about any activity you can think of. Museums, galleries, tours, clubs, etc.
6. If you are willing to make a day trip out of it, there's both mountains and beach nearby.
7. If you are seriously committed to traveling, buses to NYC can be like $18 from DC.

Hope some of that is helpful!
