## Epic Things to Do Near GMU: Break Out of the Study Grind

Stuck in the "study-sleep-repeat" cycle? Time to shake things up! Fairfax has way more cool stuff than most GMU students realize — escape rooms, cheap eats, nature spots, and hidden gems that even upperclassmen haven't tried. Best part? Most things to do near GMU are either free or student-discounted.

This guide rounds up the coolest things to do near GMU — all within 15 minutes of campus — so you can make the most of your weekends, study breaks, or post-midterm victory laps.

### 🏫 On-Campus Perks You're Probably Sleeping On
#### 🎭 Free Shows at Center for the Arts

Yep — free. Mason students can grab up to two tickets per show from the box office. From jazz to theater to international dance, this is one of the easiest ways to feel classy without dropping cash. It's literally one of the best things to do near GMU because it's ON campus.
#### 🏀 Catch a Game at EagleBank Arena

Home games are a whole vibe. Whether it's basketball, volleyball, or a campus event, your Mason ID gets you through the door. Bring friends, rep that green and gold, and scream like you're courtside at March Madness.
### 🚗 10-Minute Trips That Feel Like a Getaway
#### 🎤 Big Shows at EagleBank Arena

When the Patriots aren't playing, the arena transforms into a concert hall. Artists like Keshi, Karan Aujla, and AJR have hit this stage — and it's right on your campus. Watch for student discounts or pre-sale drops.
#### 🛍️ Mosaic District = Mini City Vibes

Mosaic is your escape from campus routines. They've got outdoor movie nights, food trucks, aesthetic coffee spots, and weekend farmers markets. It's artsy, it's photogenic, and it's perfect for dates or friend hangouts.
#### 🧠 Bonds Escape Room (Just 6 Minutes from Campus!)

Ready to challenge your brain and test your friend group? Bonds Escape Room is a local legend — over a dozen themed rooms, wild storylines, and even a live-action Game Show Arena. It's perfect for birthdays, team bonding, or just an epic night out.

### 🌳 Outdoors Without the Trek
#### 🏞️ Chill Vibes at Burke Lake Park

Need a break from screens? This park has walking trails, picnic spots, and lakeside live music during summer. It's the perfect place to reset your brain after finals or just unwind with friends. Definitely one of the most peaceful things to do near GMU.
#### ☕ Historic Walks & Bubble Tea Runs

Take a stroll through Old Town Fairfax — cozy cafés, quirky shops, and more bubble tea spots than you'll know what to do with. Pro tip: start at Kung Fu Tea, then work your way down the block.
