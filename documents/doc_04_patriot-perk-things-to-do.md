## Corner Pocket

10% Off

Host your next graduation party, birthday party, milestone, or anniversary celebration at George Mason's game room, **Corner Pocket**, located on George Mason University's campus in The Hub. Current students and faculty/staff receive a **10%** discount when booking for event rentals.

## Creative Cauldron Stage

$10 Off

Guided by the belief that creativity is a fundamental need of the human spirit, Creative Cauldron Stage is dedicated to providing affordable, enriching, and diverse experiences in the performing and visual arts to the local community.

George Mason students, faculty, staff, alumni, and retirees receive $10 off each ticket with the promo code: **MASON** at checkout. The discounts are eligible for all professional theatrical productions or any Learning Theater Ensemble productions at the regional theatre. Seating subject to availability. May not be eligible for opening or closing nights or concert series.

- Students, Faculty & Staff, Alumni, Retirees

## Escapology

20% Off

Escapology provides premium, immersive escape room experiences where participants solve puzzles, uncover clues, and complete a mission within 60 minutes. Designed for private groups, each experience features cinematic themes, engaging gameplay, and high-quality environments, making it an ideal activity for friends, families, and team-building events.

George Mason students, faculty, and staff can enjoy **20% off** all escape room games Monday through Friday. This offer excludes VIP events and birthday parties.

## Gaming Giant

5% Off

Gaming Giant specializes in trading cards, featuring an extensive selection of Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh, One Piece, and other popular titles. The store also offers a variety of accessories, food, and beverages to enhance the gaming experience. Visitors can enjoy a welcoming atmosphere, relax in the café area, and connect with fellow collectors and gamers. Customers are invited to join the Gaming Giant Discord community for updates on tournaments and special events.

Current George Mason University students, faculty, and staff receive a **5%** discount on most in-store items.

- Students, Faculty & Staff
